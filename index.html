Our Story

A muse for the modern traveller

Inspired by the merchants, artisans and storytellers who once gathered along the Straits, our hotel is a love letter to Singapore — its craftsmanship, its flavours and its effortless blend of cultures.

Every detail is considered, from hand-finished Peranakan-inspired tiles and rich teak to a personal concierge who remembers exactly how you take your kopi.
