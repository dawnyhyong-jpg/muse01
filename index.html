I · The Hotel

A muse for the modern traveller

Inspired by the merchants, artisans and storytellers who once gathered along the Straits, Straits Muse is a love letter to Singapore — its craftsmanship, its flavours and its effortless blend of cultures.

Hand-finished tiles, rich teak and softly lit courtyards set the stage; a personal concierge who remembers exactly how you take your kopi completes it.
